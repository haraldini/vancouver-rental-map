# Search-area redraw: 2026-10-21

Owner-authorized refinements and additional trial areas. These are approximate
rental-search outlines, not official neighbourhood boundaries, parcel exclusions,
road-centreline traces, or measured walking-time catchments. Descriptions remain
three short lines: Good, Bad, Important. Provenance is kept here, not in popups.

## Scope

- 37 features total: 36 Polygon search pockets and the unchanged Highway 1 LineString.
- 13 new IDs: seven split-off pockets and six additional trial pockets.
- Six existing polygons redrawn: Central Lonsdale, Kits, Dunbar, Parkgate, Moody Centre, and Cambie Village.
- The existing eastern False Creek feature is renamed Olympic Village; its geometry is unchanged.
- Seventeen other original features remain completely unchanged. No original IDs were deleted.
- No budget, bedroom, lease, or other property filters were changed.

Dunbar now has village, southern residential, and Southlands pockets. Kits has
beach-side/Kits Point and southern streets. Central Lonsdale is separated from
Grand Boulevard; Parkgate is separated from the original southern Seymour lobe.
Moody Centre/Rocky Point is separated from Suter Brook/Newport/Inlet Centre.
Douglas Park is separated from the eastern Cambie Village trial area.

Additional trial pockets: False Creek South/Charleson Park, Fraser village,
Hastings-Sunrise, Burquitlam, Lafarge Lake/Town Centre, and Austin Heights.
Coquitlam is permitted only for these trial pockets, not as blanket municipality
approval. Existing named northern exceptions and the Kerrisdale proposal remain
unchanged.

## Geometry references and method

[redraw-reference.json](redraw-reference.json) records 62 accepted BC Address
Geocoder intersections, their coordinates, match scores, IDs, and request URLs.
All accepted intersections scored 100 and matched the intended street/locality.
Rejected street-only and wrong-locality results were not used. Projected corners
are explicitly identified in the table below; they do not assert nonexistent
intersections. Straight segments between anchors approximate the selected streets.

Burquitlam uses the City of Coquitlam's Cariboo/Burquitlam community polygon,
clipped to the chosen Foster/Como Lake/Dogwood envelope. This preserves the western
municipal boundary rather than assuming Clarke Road is the city boundary.
False Creek South combines street anchors with three vertices from Vancouver's
Fairview local-area boundary, with an Anderson/Lameys Mill notch to avoid treating
Granville Island as part of the residential search pocket. It is not a copy of the
official False Creek South planning boundary. Kits uses residential street anchors,
not an exact shoreline. The Southlands pocket is deliberately conservative and
may miss viable homes elsewhere in Southlands.

- [Vancouver local-area boundary data](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/local-area-boundary/), City of Vancouver. [Open Government Licence - Vancouver](https://vancouver.ca/your-government/open-data-licence.aspx).

## Preservation and validation

Retained IDs remain stable. New split features record `derived_from`; copied KML
notes and styles retain their source meaning, while `source`, `geometry_method`,
and `boundary_accuracy` describe the new outline. Original source provenance is
retained under `original_source`. Earlier geometry remains in Git history.
Feature opacity and line widths are retained. Display colours follow the current
priority legend in both geojson.io and uMap properties.

Structural validation, ring-crossing checks, direct comparison of untouched
features, and visual inspection of each redrawn outline are performed before
publishing the proposal. Actual walks, road curves, hills, and edge-case addresses
still require individual checks. Small overlaps are possible because these are
search pockets rather than a partition: Fraser intentionally overlaps Riley Park,
and approximate existing edges may overlap adjacent pockets. Do not interpret an
intersection of two trial/core pockets as overriding the stricter property filters.
